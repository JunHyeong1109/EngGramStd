# Day 1 — Parts of Speech

**Stage:** Fundamentals (Days 1–15)

## Topic: Parts of Speech

Every English word belongs to a category called a "part of speech," which determines how it functions in a sentence. The eight main parts of speech are: noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, and interjection. Knowing these categories helps you understand sentence structure and build correct sentences. The same word can sometimes function as different parts of speech depending on context (e.g., "run" can be a verb or a noun).

## Examples

1. **The dog barked loudly.**
   - 그 개가 시끄럽게 짖었다.
   - *dog* (noun), *barked* (verb), *loudly* (adverb)

2. **She is very happy today.**
   - 그녀는 오늘 매우 행복하다.
   - *She* (pronoun), *is* (verb), *happy* (adjective), *today* (adverb)

3. **The book is on the table, and I love it.**
   - 그 책은 탁자 위에 있고, 나는 그것을 좋아한다.
   - *on* (preposition), *and* (conjunction), *it* (pronoun)
